v0.12.9: ChatBubble reports its own growth, and IconButton hovers in accent

A bubble is PLACED by its host, so a bubble that grew had no way to say so:
nothing inside a component can move the neighbour under it. The answer
overflowed the box that was meant to contain it, the transcript stopped
showing the words as they arrived, and a folded block never gave its space
back. `onHeightChanged` is that one channel, and it is the host's job to act
on it.

The measure is cached alongside the inputs that produce it - width, size,
text, expansion, copy row, header - so a streamed delta costs one layout of
ONE message instead of a re-layout of every message in the conversation.
Sixty text layouts per delta is the earlier freeze wearing a new shape.

`setBodyFontSize` and `setShowBubble` complete the component: a transcript
is READ, so the host owns the size and every bubble re-measures when it
moves, and the assistant's long answers sit on the panel's own background
while the user's own message and a note keep their chrome.

IconButton's unframed hover was a grey backdrop - and grey reads as disabled,
which teaches the user that the affordance is dead while the pointer is on
it. It is now an accent-tinted backdrop, with a blue glyph for the shapes
whose ink carries no meaning of its own; a red delete, a gold favourite and
a golden hidden eye keep the hue that means what it means.

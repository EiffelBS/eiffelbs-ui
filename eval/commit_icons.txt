feat(icons): sparkle + kebab shapes, and IconTextButton carries a label

- Shape::sparkle: the AI glyph - a big four-pointed star with concave sides
  plus a smaller companion, drawn procedurally so it recolours like every
  other glyph. Shape::star already means "favourite" in OpenTimbre's Takes
  list, so one shape must not carry two meanings in one app.
- Shape::kebab: three vertical dots, a FILLED outline rather than the stroked
  Feather style - dots under the stroke-width floor vanish at the 10 px the
  icon slot renders. It is the options affordance in front of the Assistant's
  template combo.
- IconTextButton: icon left, label glued to its right (ebs::IconButton draws
  no text and juce::DrawableButton centres the two on top of each other, so
  neither produces the pair without an override), with widthForLabel()
  measured rather than guessed and an optional frame. The label now defaults
  to empty so a glyph-only kebab button does not read as a typo in braces.

DataList.h is not part of this commit: it carries another session's edits.
